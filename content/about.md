i'm a forward deployed engineer at [Lattice Partners](https://www.linkedin.com/company/lattice-partners), an ai-first consulting firm (not the lattice hr software company). i'm based in missoula, montana — grew up here — and spend my days building ai agents for companies that are not really set up like software teams yet: small businesses, operators with legacy tools, and non-technical leadership who still need something that ships.

outside work: bikes and racing, running, and baking (usually in that order after 5).

## what does a forward deployed engineer do?

the short version: i sit with the people doing the actual work, figure out what would help them tomorrow (not in a six-month roadmap), and build the agent or automation that fits how they already operate. that means a lot of listening, a lot of spelunking in spreadsheets and email, and writing software that connects to the messy middle — not a slide deck about "ai transformation."

i still write code, still own reliability when something breaks at 4pm on a friday, and still translate between "what the model can do" and "what this business will trust."
