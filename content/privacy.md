Mahns Consulting ("we," "us," or "our") operates darraghmahns.com and provides consulting services including PropTech automation tools. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our services, particularly our Dotloop API integration tools.

---

## 1. Limited Use & Transient Data Processing

Our services function as a **pass-through automation tool**. When you use our document processing and Dotloop integration services:

- We process uploaded documents solely to extract relevant data fields and synchronize them with your Dotloop account via the Dotloop API.
- **We do not permanently retain raw document contents** or personally identifiable information (PII) after the transaction sync is completed.
- Document data is held in temporary memory or ephemeral storage only during the active processing session and is immediately purged upon completion or timeout.
- We maintain minimal transactional logs (timestamps, success/failure status) for operational purposes only, which do not contain the underlying document content or sensitive client PII.

## 2. Prohibition on AI Model Training

**Mahns Consulting does not use Customer Data, Transaction Data, or data retrieved from the Dotloop API to train, fine-tune, or improve foundation Artificial Intelligence models.**

- Your data is never fed into machine learning pipelines for model training purposes.
- We do not use your documents, loop data, contact information, or transaction details to develop, enhance, or fine-tune AI systems.
- Any AI-assisted features in our tools use pre-trained models with no customer-specific training or fine-tuning applied.

## 3. Data Sovereignty & User Ownership

**You retain full ownership of your data at all times.**

- Our access to your Dotloop account is strictly limited to the specific OAuth scopes you explicitly grant.
- We operate under a principle of **least privilege access**.
- You may revoke our access to your Dotloop account at any time through your Dotloop account settings.
- We act solely as a data processor on your behalf. You remain the data controller with complete rights to access, modify, export, or delete your data.

## 4. No Third-Party Sale of Data

Mahns Consulting **does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise monetize your personal information** to third parties, including data brokers, marketing agencies, advertisers, or lead generation services.

## 5. Information We Collect

**Information you provide:** Name, email address, OAuth credentials (securely stored and encrypted), and documents you upload for processing.

**Automatically collected:** IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, timestamps, and essential session cookies.

## 6. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information only to: provide and maintain our services, authenticate your access, communicate about service updates, detect and address technical issues, and comply with legal obligations.

## 7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share your information only: with Dotloop (via their API), with trusted service providers under strict confidentiality agreements, when required by law, or with your explicit consent.

## 8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security including TLS/SSL encryption, secure OAuth 2.0 token management, regular security audits, and access controls.

## 9. Data Retention

- **Transient Data:** Immediately deleted upon session completion.
- **Account Data:** Retained while your account is active.
- **Logs:** Retained for up to 90 days for security purposes.
- Upon account termination, personal information is deleted within 30 days.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to: access, correct, delete, and export your personal data; revoke OAuth access at any time; and object to processing for specific purposes.

To exercise these rights, [contact us](/contact). We will respond within 30 days.
